# Code of Conduct

SceneForge contributors are expected to keep technical collaboration respectful, direct, and constructive.

Acceptable participation includes giving actionable feedback, discussing tradeoffs with evidence, respecting different experience levels, and protecting private user data. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and abusive language are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports should use a private maintainer channel whenever they contain personal or security-sensitive information. Enforcement decisions should be documented and applied consistently.

This policy applies in the repository, project communication channels, and public spaces where someone represents SceneForge.
